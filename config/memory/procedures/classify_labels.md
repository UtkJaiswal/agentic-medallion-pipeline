Rules consolidated from human review sessions (procedural memory). They override general habits.
- Values are cut off at 60 characters. A cut-off phrase that names a particular object, place, room
  or symptom is "description_text", even if it is short or ends mid-word.
- A single topic word or a "<topic> issue" phrase (a utility, a system, a trade) is "category_label".
- Never use "generic_label" for a value that names one of the taxonomy categories or a recognisable
  synonym of one; generic is only for words like "various" that name no kind of work.
- Thermostats, room temperature, humidity and units whose coils freeze are hvac, not electrical.
- Furniture and fixture requests are general_maintenance.
