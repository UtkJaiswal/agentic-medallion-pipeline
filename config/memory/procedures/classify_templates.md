Rules consolidated from human review sessions (procedural memory). They override general habits.
- A description that is only a category word or abbreviation names that category; never "unknown".
- Insects, rodents and birds are pest_control wherever they are seen (kitchens and washrooms included).
- Blocked drains, leaking or overflowing fixtures are plumbing, even in a cleaner's cupboard.
- Stains and requests for professional or deep cleaning are janitorial, whatever the surface.
- Lighting faults and requests for more sockets are electrical, not general maintenance.
- Expired, missing or faulty fire equipment is a safety hazard (is_safety_hazard = true).
